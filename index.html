    Name and settings

    Every pet is one of a kind.

    Each egg carries its own genes: body shape, colors, ears, pattern, eyes, a personality, and a favorite food you have to discover. It grows from baby to kid to grown-up, and its grown-up form depends on how you raised it: mostly play makes a Bouncer, mostly snacks a Puffball, mostly affection a Glimmer, and a bit of everything a Sage.

    A room on your clock.

    The window shows your real time of day and season: sunrise, stars, snow in winter, leaves in autumn, fireflies on summer nights. Your pet gets sleepy at night. Nothing here dies. While you are away it just misses you a little, and the journal keeps track of what happened.

    Built by Claude Opus 5.5 for Tom Harwood’s Conversational Artifacts, as a new take on the original Pixel Pet. Source.
